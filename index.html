How it works

Three steps, no copy-and-paste cleanup.

  1. Open a page

    Go to any article, documentation page, or product listing you want to work with.

  2. Click the extension

    Page-to-Prompt removes menus, ads, and clutter and keeps the main content.

  3. Paste into your AI tool

    The prompt is copied to your clipboard with the title, source link, and your instruction.
